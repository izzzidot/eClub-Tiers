Fine print, upfront

Three things worth saying clearly.

01

Onboarding

A one-time fee applies — $150 Starter, $300 Growth, $500 Signature. Covers safari brand audit, itinerary analysis, tone-of-voice document, channel setup, and template system. Month one is foundational; this keeps it separate from ongoing work.

02

Minimum term

Three months on Starter. Six months on Growth and Signature. Social media compounds — building traveler trust and safari interest takes time, and no honest agency promises instant overnight bookings. Commitment is priced in.

03

Ad budget

Advertising budget is billed separately. The packages cover full setup, targeting optimization, visual creatives, and campaign management. You maintain direct control over your budget, paid directly to platforms.
